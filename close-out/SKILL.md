# Close-Out

When I ask you to close out, bring this session to a clean, finished stopping point. The task is done, or can be finished right now. Your job is to make the result trustworthy: finish the agreed work, check it, bring my files and status up to date, and tell me plainly what is actually done.

This is the partner to Handoff. Use Handoff when real work still remains and you are moving to a new chat. Use Close-Out when the work is finished.

## What to do

1. **Finish the agreed scope.** Complete what we set out to do. Do not start new, unrelated work or add improvements I did not ask for.
2. **Check it.** Verify the result however you can: re-read it, test it, walk through it. Only tell me something works if you actually confirmed it. If you could not check something, say so.
3. **Bring my files up to date.** Update anything that should reflect the finished work: the notes, the status line, the index of what lives where. Someone opening these files later should see the true, current state.
4. **Clean up.** Remove scratch or temporary files this task created and no longer needs. Do not touch my existing files, folders, or anything you did not create.
5. **Summarize honestly.** Write a short, plain summary: what is done, what you actually checked, and anything still open or worth knowing.

## Rules

- Do not label unfinished work "done." If something is blocked or incomplete, say so plainly, and if real work remains, write a Handoff instead.
- Distinguish what you verified from what you assumed.
- Do not delete my files, notes, or earlier work. Only clean up the disposable things this task created.
- Do not make unrelated changes while tidying up.
- Do not send, post, publish, or share anything outside my own files unless I explicitly ask.

## Quality check before you finish

- The agreed task is genuinely complete, or the blocker is clearly stated.
- My files and status reflect reality, not what we hoped.
- Anything you did not actually verify is labeled as an assumption.
- The summary is short, honest, and free of unrelated commentary.

If the task is not actually finished, tell me what is missing instead of closing it out.
